# Disassembly

The inspected Bublcam has three small Phillips screws around the tripod socket. Opening the bottom exposes the battery and electronics.

The aluminium camera/lens sections use tiny **spring/roll pins** as hinge axes. On the inspected unit they appeared ~0.8 mm diameter.

Recommended approach:
1. Support the aluminium locally around the pin.
2. Use a small flat-ended punch slightly smaller than the pin.
3. Tap gently.
4. Avoid a sharp needle point that can expand the spring pin.
5. Keep pins organised.

After pin removal the camera unfolds like a flower and exposes the central PCB, flexes, battery and test pads.

**Do not loosen camera/lens alignment mounts.** Factory calibration depends on their geometry.
